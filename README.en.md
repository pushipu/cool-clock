# Cool Clock

The iconic, super-stylish clock everyone knows. Now a screensaver for macOS and Windows.

Both releases are previews. On Windows, automatic activation after inactivity and behavior after locking the PC still need testing on everyday PCs. The installers are not yet signed with a publisher certificate, so your system may show a warning the first time you open them.

## Setup and sound

Run the installer and choose whether to enable sound. The wizard then shows you how to set Cool Clock as your screensaver and choose how long it waits before starting.

On Mac, a guide appears beside System Settings. It also helps you turn off the large macOS clock, which can overlap the dial. On Windows, the guide is built into the installer, with buttons that open screensaver and power settings.

Sound is off by default. You can enable it during installation or later in the screensaver settings. The music and beeps at the end of each minute are synchronized with the second hand. The background fits your screen without black bars.
